If a bat and a ball cost $1.10 in total, and the bat costs $1.00 more than the ball,
how much does the ball cost?

Then: three people check into a $30 room, each pays $10. The clerk realizes the room
is $25, gives $5 to the bellboy to return. The bellboy keeps $2 and gives $1 back to
each guest. Each guest paid $9 = $27, plus the bellboy's $2 = $29. Where is the missing dollar?
